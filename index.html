Our Love Story

We are overjoyed to gather our closest family and friends in sunny South Florida. From snowy trails in Rocky Mountain National Park and Red Rocks to historic gardens in Miami, Key West refuges, and cozy evenings at home with our cat Ellie, every chapter has led us to this lakeside celebration.

Chapter I • First Spark

Two paths grounded in a love for landscapes, maps, design, and nature crossed in Florida and turned every weekend into an adventure.

Chapter II • Memory Lane

Exploring mountain peaks, art museums, natural springs, and botanical gardens together across Colorado and Florida.

Chapter III • Forever

Saying our vows beside the water before heading off on our Oregon and California coastal honeymoon road trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 10:30 AM to stroll down the garden path past our welcome painting and take your seat before the 11:00 AM ceremony.

Is the ceremony outdoors?

Yes, vows take place on the lakeside lawn at Galuppi's followed by a covered patio and indoor dining reception.

Can I wear Indian or Western attire?

Both are warmly encouraged! Garden formal dresses, suits, sarees, lehengas, and kurtas in earthy floral tones all fit right in.

Is parking available on site?

Complimentary parking is available directly in front of Galuppi's at 1103 N Federal Hwy.

Transportation

Travel guidance for guests arriving from across the country and internationally from India.

By Air

Fly into Fort Lauderdale International (FLL), located 20 minutes from Galuppi's, or Palm Beach International (PBI), 35 minutes north.

By Road & Rail

Easily accessible via I-95 to Sample Rd / US-1 in Pompano Beach, with Brightline rail stations in West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, and Fort Lauderdale.
