Attire & Aesthetic

Garden Formal & Cultural Elegance

We invite our guests to dress in Garden Formal or Indian Festive attire that complements our romantic botanical palette. Notice the bridal combination of structured ivory satin, cathedral veil drama, statement earrings, and traditional Indian henna extending to the elbows.

  • • For Women: Midi or floor-length dresses, sarees, or lehengas in soft earth tones, sage, taupe, blush, or floral prints. Block heels or wedges are recommended for the ceremony lawn.
  • • For Men: Suits, dress trousers with blazers, or classic kurtas and bandhgalas in olive, taupe, charcoal, or linen neutrals.

For Out-Of-Town Guests

Travel, Stay & Local Guide

Everything you need for a smooth trip to South Florida in January.

01 • Getting Here

Airports & Transit

Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International (FLL): Located 20 minutes south of Galuppi's and Pompano Beach.

Palm Beach International (PBI): Located 35 minutes north in West Palm Beach, ideal if you are staying near our home base.

Brightline high-speed rail also connects West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Fort Lauderdale, and Miami.

02 • Accommodations

Where To Stay

We recommend booking near Pompano Beach for quick venue access, or in West Palm Beach and Boca Raton for coastal sightseeing:

  • • Fort Lauderdale Marriott Pompano Beach Resort: Oceanfront, 10 mins from venue.
  • • Hilton Garden Inn Boca Raton: Central between West Palm Beach and Pompano.
  • • Canopy by Hilton West Palm Beach: Vibrant downtown stay near the waterfront.
03 • Our Favorites

See, Do & Dine

  • • Gardens & Art: Stroll through Morikami Japanese Gardens in Delray Beach, Boca Raton Museum of Art, or Vizcaya Museum & Gardens in Miami.
  • • Nature & Coast: Wakodahatchee Wetlands in Delray Beach or a sunny walk along the Pompano Beach Pier.
  • • Where To Eat: Chhauka in West Palm Beach for authentic Indian flavors, or coastal dining along Atlantic Avenue in Delray.

Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I arrive for the ceremony? +

Please arrive 25 to 30 minutes prior to the ceremony start time so you can park comfortably, walk down the garden brick path past our welcome signage, and find your seat by the lakeside arch.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors? +

The ceremony takes place outdoors on the lawn overlooking the water and greens at Galuppi's, followed by a covered patio and indoor reception. January weather in South Florida is typically sunny and mild in the low 70s (°F).

Can I bring a plus-one or children? +

Because we are hosting an intimate daytime celebration of approximately 45 guests, we are only able to accommodate the guests formally named on your invitation.

Can we wear Indian traditional attire or Western formal wear? +

Both are warmly welcomed and encouraged! Whether you choose a garden formal dress or suit, or a saree, lehenga, or kurta in our earthy floral palette, we want you to feel celebratory and comfortable.
