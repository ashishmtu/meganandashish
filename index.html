Our Love Story

Their paths first crossed in West Palm Beach, where they both worked and shared a love for the city, his through mapping and hers through landscape design. Their first date was at the serene Morikami Museum and Japanese Gardens during the beautiful month of December. What began as a connection soon grew into something deeply meaningful. One year later, they are so excited to begin the next chapter of their lives together!

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 03:30 PM to stroll down the garden path past our welcome painting and take your seat before the 04:00 PM ceremony.

Is the ceremony outdoors?

Yes, vows take place on the lakeside lawn at Galuppi's followed by a covered patio and indoor dining reception.

Can I wear Indian or Western attire?

Both are warmly encouraged! Garden formal dresses, suits, sarees, lehengas, and kurtas in earthy floral tones all fit right in.

Is parking available on site?

Complimentary parking is available directly in front of Galuppi's at 1103 N Federal Hwy.
